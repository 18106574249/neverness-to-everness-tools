NTE Characters - All Resonators Database

All 16 launch characters for Neverness to Everness. Updated: June 11, 2026

Neverness to Everness (NTE) is an urban fantasy open-world RPG by Hotta Studio, set in the supernatural city of Hethereau. The game features a diverse cast of characters called Resonators, each with unique abilities, elements, and combat roles. This database covers every character with build recommendations, team synergy analysis, and pull priority guidance.

S-Class (UR / SSR)

The highest rarity characters with the strongest base stats and most impactful abilities. Priority pulls for any account.

Nanally

UR - DPS - Limited banner (Launch)

Nanally is the premier launch DPS with exceptional single-target damage and AoE clearing capability. Her ultimate ability deals massive burst damage and provides team-wide buffs. Best team: Nanally + Sakiri + any healer. Top priority pull for new accounts.

Hotori

S-Class - Solid DPS - Current banner (May 13 - Jun 3)

Hotori excels in sustained damage output with a unique stacking mechanic that increases damage over time. Strong in both boss fights and wave clearing. Best team: Hotori + Fadia + healer.

Sakiri

S-Class - Support / Buffer - Universal team slot

The best universal support in NTE. Sakiri provides team-wide ATK buffs, damage amplification, and energy regeneration. Fits into literally any team composition. Priority pull for all players.

Lacrimosa

S-Class - DPS - Coming June 4

Upcoming limited DPS with unique lifesteal mechanics. Early testing suggests high survivability with competitive damage output. Save pulls if you need a self-sustaining DPS.

Chaos

S-Class - DPS - Coming June 19

Chaos specializes in AoE damage with crowd control effects. Expected to excel in wave-based content and multi-target encounters.

Daffodil

S-Class - DPS - Standard banner

Reliable standard banner DPS with consistent damage output. Great for F2P players who get her from the beginner banner or lose 50/50.

Fadia

S-Class - Sub-DPS - Standard banner

Excellent off-field damage dealer with debuff capabilities. Reduces enemy defense, making her valuable in any team as a sub-DPS slot.

A-Class (SR)

Solid mid-rarity characters that can perform well with proper investment. Many are available for free through story progression.

Haniel

A-Class - Support / Healer - Current banner rate-up

Best A-Class healer with consistent team healing and cleanse effects. Rate-up on current banner makes her easy to obtain. Excellent budget healer option.

Baicang

A-Class - DPS - Standard banner

Solid A-Class DPS with straightforward gameplay. Good for new players learning combat mechanics. Falls off in late-game compared to S-Class options.

Chiz

A-Class - Support - FREE from story

Free story character with unique shield mechanics. Provides team-wide damage reduction and can tank for the party. Great for difficult boss fights.

Zero / Esper Zero

A-Class - DPS - FREE from story

Free story DPS with teleportation abilities. High mobility makes him fun to play and effective in open-world exploration. Decent damage for a free unit.

Jiuyuan

A-Class - DPS - Standard banner

Ranged DPS with good single-target damage. Requires positioning skill but rewards with high sustained output. Good for boss fights.

Adler

A-Class - Support - FREE from story

Free support with energy battery capabilities. Helps the team use ultimates more frequently. Valuable in any team composition.

Hathor

A-Class - Sub-DPS - Standard banner

Off-field damage dealer with elemental application. Good for enabling elemental reactions and providing supplemental damage.

B-Class (R)

Entry-level characters available for free. Useful for early game but quickly outscaled.

Aurelia

B-Class - DPS - FREE from story

Starter DPS character. Serviceable for early game content but should be replaced once you obtain A-Class or higher DPS units.

Mint

B-Class - DPS - FREE from story

Starter character with basic combat abilities. Use for story progression until you pull better characters.

Pull Priority Guide

  • New players: Pull for Sakiri first - universal support that fits every team.
  • DPS priority: Nanally (limited) > Hotori (current) > Lacrimosa (upcoming June 4).
  • Free characters: Chiz, Zero, and Adler are all viable and available through story progression.
  • Standard banner: Daffodil and Fadia are the best standard 5-star pulls.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best character in NTE?

Nanally (UR) is currently the highest damage ceiling character in the game, excelling in both single-target and AoE content. However, Sakiri is arguably the most valuable overall due to her universal support capabilities that benefit any team composition.

Should I pull for limited characters or save?

For new players, we recommend pulling Sakiri first (universal support), then saving for the next limited DPS that fits your team. Limited characters are generally stronger than standard banner options, but the pity system means you need up to 160 pulls to guarantee a featured character.

Can I clear content with A-Class characters?

Yes. A-Class characters like Haniel (healer) and Chiz (shielder) are fully viable for most content. The game is designed so that skilled play with lower-rarity characters can clear endgame content, though it may take more effort and optimal builds.

How do team compositions work?

Teams consist of 3 characters: a Main DPS, a Sub-DPS or Support, and a Healer or Shielder. The best teams synergize through elemental reactions and buff stacking. For example, Nanally + Sakiri + Haniel covers damage, buffs, and healing for most content.
