Abyss Surges — 5-Star Gauntlets

5-StarGauntlets

Abyss Surges is the top gauntlet in Neverness to Everness, with a stacking DMG bonus that grows with each Resonance Skill use. Its design rewards active skill rotations, making it the natural best-in-slot for gauntlet users like Zero, whose mobile playstyle cycles skills frequently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Abyss Surges good?

Yes. It is the top gauntlet with a stacking DMG bonus (10% per Resonance Skill use, up to 3 stacks).

Who should use Abyss Surges?

Zero is the primary user. Any gauntlet DPS that cycles skills frequently benefits.

What is a good F2P alternative?

Marcato, the 4-star gauntlet with Energy Regen, is the main budget option for supports; DPS players may prefer higher-rarity options.
