Autumntrace — 4-Star Greatsword

4-StarGreatsword

Autumntrace is a solid 4-star greatsword with a Crit DMG secondary stat, offering strong damage potential for a budget weapon. Its passive increases ATK after taking damage, which suits frontline characters like Baicang who naturally absorb hits while dealing heavy blows.

Weaknesses & Considerations

Who Should Build Autumntrace

Autumntrace is best suited for players who enjoy the character's playstyle and want a flexible team member. If you are filling a character slot for endgame content, Autumntrace is a dependable choice — especially for accounts without a limited-banner alternative for this role.

Progression Priority

Prioritize its team synergy first, then upgrade the recommended weapon and echoes. Practice the rotation in lower-stakes content before taking it into endgame modes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Autumntrace good?

Yes. It offers a Crit DMG substat and an ATK-boosting passive, making it a strong 4-star greatsword.

Who should use Autumntrace?

Baicang is the primary user. Any greatsword DPS that takes hits benefits from its passive.

What is the best greatsword?

Lustrous Razor (5-star) is the best; Autumntrace is the leading F2P alternative.
