Zero — A Main DPS

AMain DPSFREE from story

Zero (also called Esper Zero) is a free story DPS with teleportation abilities. His high mobility makes him fun to play and effective in open-world exploration, while his decent damage output makes him a serviceable main DPS for a free unit.

Who Should Build Zero

Zero is best suited for players who need a dependable damage dealer and are willing to learn its rotation. If you are filling a main dps slot for endgame content, Zero is a dependable choice — especially for accounts without a limited-banner alternative for this role.
