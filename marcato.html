Marcato — 4-Star Gauntlets

4-StarGauntlets

Marcato is a utility 4-star gauntlet designed for support characters. Its Energy Regen secondary stat and energy restoration passive keep skills and ultimates charging faster, making it the go-to budget option for gauntlet-wielding supports and enabling more frequent burst rotations for the whole team.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Marcato good for supports?

Yes. Its Energy Regen substat and Concerto Energy restoration passive make it the best budget gauntlet for support characters.

Who should use Marcato?

Support characters that use Gauntlets, such as shield and energy battery units.

Is Marcato good for DPS?

No. DPS characters should prefer Abyss Surges (5-star) or higher-damage options.
