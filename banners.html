NTE Banner Schedule (2026) - Current & Upcoming

Current and upcoming convene banners with pity system guide.

Neverness to Everness uses a gacha system called "Convene" for obtaining characters and weapons. Limited banners feature rate-up characters with a 50/50 pity system. Standard banners use a separate currency and have no time limit. Plan your pulls carefully to maximize your chances of getting the characters you want.

Pity System Explained

  • Soft pity: Starts at 65 pulls. Rate increases gradually until hard pity.
  • Hard pity: Guaranteed 5-star at 80 pulls (limited) or 90 pulls (standard).
  • 50/50: First 5-star on limited banner has 50% chance to be the featured character.
  • Guarantee: If you lose 50/50, next 5-star is guaranteed to be the featured character.
  • Pity carries over: Pity count transfers between limited banners of the same type.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the pity system work?

NTE uses a 50/50 pity system for limited banners. Your first 5-star pull has a 50% chance of being the featured character. If you lose the 50/50, your next 5-star is guaranteed to be the featured character. Soft pity starts at 65 pulls, with hard pity at 80 pulls.

How many pulls does it take to guarantee a character?

Worst case: 160 pulls (lose 50/50 at hard pity, then win guaranteed at hard pity). Average case: 80-100 pulls. With monthly pass and daily play, free-to-play players can save approximately 60-80 pulls per patch.

Should I pull on the weapon banner?

Only if you have spare currency after securing the characters you want. Weapon banners have worse pity value than character banners. Signature weapons provide 15-25% damage improvement but are not required for clearing content.

What is the best pull strategy for new players?

1) Pull Beginner Banner for guaranteed 5-star within 50 pulls. 2) Save for Sakiri (universal support). 3) Pull the next limited DPS that fits your team. 4) Avoid weapon banners until you have a solid character roster. 5) Never pull on standard banner with premium currency.
