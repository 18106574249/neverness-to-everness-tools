NTE Banner Schedule (2026) - Current & Upcoming

Current and upcoming convene banners with pity system guide.

Neverness to Everness uses a gacha system called "Convene" for obtaining characters and weapons. Limited banners feature rate-up characters with a 50/50 pity system. Standard banners use a separate currency and have no time limit. Plan your pulls carefully to maximize your chances of getting the characters you want.

Pity System Explained

  • Soft pity: Starts at 65 pulls. Rate increases gradually until hard pity.
  • Hard pity: Guaranteed 5-star at 80 pulls (limited) or 90 pulls (standard).
  • 50/50: First 5-star on limited banner has 50% chance to be the featured character.
  • Guarantee: If you lose 50/50, next 5-star is guaranteed to be the featured character.
  • Pity carries over: Pity count transfers between limited banners of the same type.
