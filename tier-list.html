NTE Tier List (2026) - Best Characters Ranked

Updated: June 11, 2026 | Based on endgame content and team synergy.

S-Tier A-Tier B-Tier

This tier list evaluates all Neverness to Everness characters based on their performance in endgame content, team synergy value, and overall account impact. Rankings consider both damage output and utility - a support that enables top-tier DPS can rank higher than a selfish DPS with mediocre numbers.

S-Tier - Meta Defining

Nanally

UR Main DPS

Highest damage ceiling in the game. Exceptional in both single-target and AoE. Limited banner exclusive.

Sakiri

S-Class Support

Best universal support. Team-wide buffs, energy regen, and damage amplification. Fits any team.

Hotori

S-Class Main DPS

Excellent sustained DPS with stacking mechanic. Strong in all content types. Currently available.

Fadia

S-Class Sub-DPS

Best off-field damage with defense shred. Universal sub-DPS slot. Standard banner available.

A-Tier - Excellent Performers

Daffodil

S-Class Main DPS

Reliable standard banner DPS. Consistent damage, easy to build. Great for F2P.

Haniel

A-Class Healer

Best budget healer. Currently rate-up. Cleanse and consistent healing.

Chiz

A-Class Support

Free shield support. Team damage reduction. Excellent for difficult content.

Zero

A-Class DPS

Free mobile DPS. Good for exploration and story. Decent damage for a free unit.

B-Tier - Solid & Viable

Baicang

A-Class DPS

Good starter DPS. Falls off in late-game. Replace when you get S-Class DPS.

Jiuyuan

A-Class DPS

Ranged DPS with positioning requirements. Rewarding when played well.

Adler

A-Class Support

Free energy battery. Helps team ult more often. Good utility value.

Hathor

A-Class Sub-DPS

Off-field elemental application. Good for reaction teams.

How We Rank

  • Endgame performance - Can they clear the hardest content consistently?
  • Team flexibility - Do they need specific teammates or can they slot anywhere?
  • Investment requirement - How much investment to reach their potential?
  • Future-proofing - Will they remain relevant as new characters release?

Frequently Asked Questions

How are characters ranked in the tier list?

Our tier list evaluates characters based on endgame performance, team synergy value, and overall account impact. We consider damage output, utility, ease of use, and how well a character enables other team members. Rankings are based on community consensus and testing.

Why is a support ranked higher than a DPS?

Supports like Sakiri provide team-wide buffs that multiply the effectiveness of your entire team. A support that enables top-tier DPS output can have more account impact than a selfish DPS with mediocre numbers. Tier lists value overall contribution over raw damage.

Will the tier list change with new patches?

Yes. Balance patches, new characters, and new content modes can shift rankings. We update the tier list after each major patch and whenever significant balance changes occur. Check the update date at the top of the page.

Should I only use S-Tier characters?

No. A-Tier and B-Tier characters are viable for all content with proper builds and team compositions. Tier lists reflect optimal performance, not viability. Build the characters you enjoy playing - skilled play often matters more than tier ranking.
