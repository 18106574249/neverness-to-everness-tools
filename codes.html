Frequently Asked Questions

How do I redeem NTE codes?

Open Neverness to Everness, go to Settings > Other > Redeem Code, enter the code, and confirm. You can also use the official website redemption page. Rewards are sent to your in-game mailbox.

Why is my code not working?

Codes may have expired, reached their redemption limit, or been entered incorrectly. Check the date the code was added - codes typically expire within 1-2 patches. Make sure you're entering the code exactly as shown, with correct capitalization.

What do NTE codes give?

Redemption codes typically provide premium currency (Annulith, Fons), upgrade materials (Rising Hunter Guides, DynamiK), cosmetic items (Dyes), and occasionally limited-time items. The rewards vary depending on whether the code is from a livestream, social media event, or special promotion.

How often are new codes released?

New codes are typically released during livestreams, patch updates, social media campaigns, and special events. We monitor all official channels and update our code list within hours of any new release.
