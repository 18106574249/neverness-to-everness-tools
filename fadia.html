Available on the standard banner, she is a universal sub-DPS pick for both DPS and sustain teams.

Fadia Analysis & Verdict

Strengths

  • strong utility that lifts the whole team, not just personal damage
  • accessible for free-to-play players and easy to build around
  • rewards mastery with consistent, sustained output

Weaknesses & Considerations

  • as an on-field damage dealer, uptime matters — plan a rotation and support setup to get full value
  • team and gear requirements should be reviewed before committing resources

Who Should Build Fadia

Fadia is best suited for players who want consistent off-field damage and debuffs alongside a main carry. If you are filling a sub-dps slot for endgame content, Fadia is a dependable choice — especially for accounts without a limited-banner alternative for this role.
