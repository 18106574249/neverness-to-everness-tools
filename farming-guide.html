NTE Farming Guide (2026)

Stamina efficiency, best domains, and material farming - updated June 21, 2026.

Neverness to Everness has multiple resource systems that compete for your daily stamina (Fons/Thermae). This guide ranks the most efficient uses of your stamina at every stage of the game, so you never waste a drop.

Domain Efficiency Rankings

Priority Domain Materials When to Farm
1st Simulated Resonance Talent/Ability upgrade materials Always - talents are permanent power
2nd Weapon Forge Weapon ascension materials After ascending your main DPS weapon
3rd Cipher Fields Echo/Artifact EXP + Drop rate Account level 40+ (gold Echoes)
4th Resonance Trials Character ascension materials When leveling a new character
5th Credit Domain Fons/Gold Only when low on funds

Material Farming by Type

Character Ascension Materials

Each character needs specific materials to ascend past level caps. These are obtained from world bosses (cost stamina) and Resonance Trials:

• World bosses drop unique ascension materials (e.g., Phantom Cores, Abyss Shards)
• Each boss costs ~60 stamina per kill
• You need ~46 boss materials for full ascension (1-90)
• Tip: Check which boss drops what before spending stamina

Ascension gems (Sliver → Fragment → Stone) come from farming domains that rotate daily.

Talent/Ability Materials

Obtained from Simulated Resonance domain. Like other games, the available material types rotate daily. Each run costs ~60 stamina.

• Prioritize your main DPS talents first
• Supports can stop at talent level 6-8
• DPS characters benefit from max talents (level 10)
• Check the daily rotation to farm what your current team needs

Echo/Artifact Farming

Echoes are the gear/artifact system in NTE. They provide set bonuses and stat sticks for your characters.

• Early game: Use any Echoes with correct main stats & set bonuses
• Mid game (40+): Farm Cipher Fields for gold Echoes with optimized substats
• Don't waste EXP on purple Echoes past level 12
• Save your best Echo XP materials for gold Echo pieces

Check our Echo Database for which sets each character needs.

Overworld Farming (Free)

These materials are free - no stamina cost:

• Local specialties - respawn daily, collect for ascension
• Enemy drops - materials from regular mobs in the overworld
• Chests & puzzles - one-time but provide significant materials
• Mining nodes - ore for weapon crafting

Set a farming route covering 5-6 key materials and run it as a quick daily loop.

Stamina Strategy by Account Level
