Lustrous Razor — 5-Star Greatsword

5-StarGreatsword

Lustrous Razor is the top greatsword in Neverness to Everness, offering a stacking ATK buff that rewards sustained field time. With each hit adding 8% ATK up to five stacks, it strongly favors characters who stay on-field and attack frequently, like Daffodil and Baicang. Its high base ATK and ATK% secondary stat make it a straightforward, powerful upgrade.

Lustrous Razor Analysis & Verdict

Strengths

  • strong performance ceiling — widely considered a top pick for its role
  • strong utility that lifts the whole team, not just personal damage
  • rewards mastery with consistent, sustained output

Weaknesses & Considerations

  • requires thoughtful gearing to feel impactful — prioritize the recommended stats over raw level investment
  • team and gear requirements should be reviewed before committing resources

Who Should Build Lustrous Razor

Lustrous Razor is best suited for players who enjoy the character's playstyle and want a flexible team member. If you are filling a character slot for endgame content, Lustrous Razor is a dependable choice — especially for accounts without a limited-banner alternative for this role.

Progression Priority

Prioritize its team synergy first, then upgrade the recommended weapon and echoes. Practice the rotation in lower-stakes content before taking it into endgame modes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Lustrous Razor good?

Yes. It is the top greatsword with a stacking ATK buff (8% per hit, up to 5 stacks) that rewards sustained field time.

Who should use Lustrous Razor?

Daffodil and Baicang are the primary users. Any greatsword DPS that attacks frequently benefits.

What is a good F2P alternative?

Autumntrace, the 4-star greatsword with Crit DMG, is the best budget option.
