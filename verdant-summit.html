Verdant Summit — 5-Star Sword

5-StarSword

Verdant Summit is widely considered the best sword in Neverness to Everness. Its combination of a high base ATK, a Crit DMG secondary stat, and a versatile passive that boosts both raw damage and post-skill ATK makes it the top choice for sword-wielding DPS characters. It is the signature-grade pick for Nanally and Hotori, and its passive uptime is easy to maintain in standard rotations.

Verdant Summit Analysis & Verdict

Strengths

  • strong performance ceiling — widely considered a top pick for its role
  • accessible for free-to-play players and easy to build around
  • rewards mastery with consistent, sustained output

Weaknesses & Considerations

  • requires thoughtful gearing to feel impactful — prioritize the recommended stats over raw level investment
  • team and gear requirements should be reviewed before committing resources

Who Should Build Verdant Summit

Verdant Summit is best suited for players who enjoy the character's playstyle and want a flexible team member. If you are filling a character slot for endgame content, Verdant Summit is a dependable choice — especially for accounts without a limited-banner alternative for this role.

Progression Priority

Prioritize its team synergy first, then upgrade the recommended weapon and echoes. Practice the rotation in lower-stakes content before taking it into endgame modes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Verdant Summit worth pulling?

Yes, for sword DPS mains. It is the best sword in the game with a Crit DMG substat and a passive that boosts all DMG and ATK after using Resonance Skill.

Who should use Verdant Summit?

Nanally and Hotori benefit most. Other sword DPS characters can also use it as their best-in-slot.

What is a good F2P alternative?

The 4-star Emerald of Genesis is the strongest budget sword option.
