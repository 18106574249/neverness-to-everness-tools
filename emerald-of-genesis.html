Emerald of Genesis — 4-Star Sword

4-StarSword

Emerald of Genesis is the premier 4-star sword in Neverness to Everness and the strongest F2P option for sword users. Its Crit Rate secondary stat helps reach crit thresholds, while the passive boosts Energy Regen and ATK after using Resonance Skill, improving both burst uptime and damage. It is a reliable choice for any sword-wielding DPS.

Emerald of Genesis Analysis & Verdict

Strengths

  • strong performance ceiling — widely considered a top pick for its role
  • reliable damage in single-target and boss encounters
  • strong utility that lifts the whole team, not just personal damage

Weaknesses & Considerations

  • requires thoughtful gearing to feel impactful — prioritize the recommended stats over raw level investment
  • team and gear requirements should be reviewed before committing resources

Who Should Build Emerald of Genesis

Emerald of Genesis is best suited for players who enjoy the character's playstyle and want a flexible team member. If you are filling a character slot for endgame content, Emerald of Genesis is a dependable choice — especially for accounts without a limited-banner alternative for this role.

Progression Priority

Prioritize its team synergy first, then upgrade the recommended weapon and echoes. Practice the rotation in lower-stakes content before taking it into endgame modes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Emerald of Genesis worth leveling?

Yes. It is the strongest 4-star sword with Crit Rate and a useful ATK/ER passive, making it an excellent F2P option.

Who should use Emerald of Genesis?

Any sword DPS character, especially F2P players who do not have Verdant Summit.

How does it compare to Verdant Summit?

Verdant Summit deals more damage, but Emerald of Genesis performs within a reasonable margin for a 4-star and is far easier to obtain.
