NTE Daily Checklist - Daily Tasks & Waveplate Tracker

Never miss dailies, waveplates, or event rewards. All progress saved locally in your browser.

Completing daily tasks in Neverness to Everness is the most efficient way to earn premium currency, upgrade materials, and account XP. This checklist covers all daily activities with estimated time requirements. Check off tasks as you complete them - your progress is saved automatically in your browser.

Daily Tasks (Reset: 4:00 AM Server Time)

Daily Commissions (4)

Reward: 60 Premium Currency + Account XP

Complete 4 daily commissions from the Adventurer's Guild. Each takes 2-3 minutes. Time: ~10 min. Highest priority daily task.

Spend Waveplates (180)

Reward: Upgrade materials + Account XP

Spend your daily waveplates on boss fights, domains, or material farming. Time: ~15 min. Never let waveplates cap at 240.

Daily Check-in

Reward: Various (HoYolab / in-game)

Claim daily login rewards from the in-game event page and any external check-in platforms. Time: ~2 min.

Expedition Dispatch

Reward: Materials + Mora-equivalent

Send characters on expeditions for passive material gathering. Collect and re-dispatch daily. Time: ~1 min.

Weekly Tasks (Reset: Monday 4:00 AM)

Weekly Bosses (3)

Reward: Boss materials + Weapon billets

Defeat 3 weekly bosses for rare upgrade materials. First 3 clears have reduced waveplate cost. Time: ~15 min.

Battle Pass Missions

Reward: Battle Pass XP

Complete weekly battle pass missions for BP levels. Most complete naturally through daily play. Time: Passive.

Abyss / Endgame Mode

Reward: Premium Currency (bi-weekly reset)

Clear endgame content for premium currency rewards. Resets every 2 weeks. Time: ~30 min.

Daily Routine (Estimated: 30 min)

  1. Login & claim check-in rewards (2 min)
  2. Collect expedition rewards & re-dispatch (1 min)
  3. Complete 4 daily commissions (10 min)
  4. Spend 180 waveplates on needed materials (15 min)
  5. Check event page for limited-time tasks (2 min)

On Monday/Wednesday/Friday, add weekly boss clears (+15 min). On endgame reset days, add Abyss runs (+30 min).
