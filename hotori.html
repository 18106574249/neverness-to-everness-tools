Hotori Analysis & Verdict

Strengths

  • effective in multi-target and wave-based content
  • reliable damage in single-target and boss encounters
  • rewards mastery with consistent, sustained output

Weaknesses & Considerations

  • as an on-field damage dealer, uptime matters — plan a rotation and support setup to get full value
  • team and gear requirements should be reviewed before committing resources

Who Should Build Hotori

Hotori is best suited for players who need a dependable damage dealer and are willing to learn its rotation. If you are filling a main dps slot for endgame content, Hotori is a dependable choice — especially for accounts without a limited-banner alternative for this role.

Progression Priority

Prioritize its team synergy first, then upgrade the recommended verdant summit and echoes. In combat, Her stacking mechanic rewards long field time. Keep her on-field to maintain stacks, and use Fadia's defense shred to amplify her damage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Hotori a good main DPS?

Yes. Hotori offers excellent sustained DPS through her stacking mechanic and is strong in both boss fights and wave clearing content.

Should I pull Hotori or save for Lacrimosa?

If you need a reliable DPS now, Hotori is a strong choice. If you prefer a self-sustaining lifesteal playstyle, Lacrimosa (coming June 4) may suit you better.
