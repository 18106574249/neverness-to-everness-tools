Nanally — UR Main DPS

URMain DPSLimited banner (Launch)

Nanally is the premier launch DPS of Neverness to Everness, celebrated for exceptional single-target damage and powerful AoE clearing capability. Her ultimate ability deals massive burst damage while providing team-wide buffs, making her the highest damage ceiling character at launch. As a limited banner exclusive, she requires deliberate pull planning, but rewards investment with top-tier performance across all content types.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Nanally worth pulling?

Yes. Nanally has the highest damage ceiling at launch and excels in both single-target and AoE content. As a limited banner exclusive, she is the top priority for new accounts.

What weapon does Nanally use?

Nanally uses a Sword. Her best-in-slot is Verdant Summit, with the 4-star Emerald of Genesis as a solid F2P alternative.
