Lacrimosa Analysis & Verdict

Strengths

  • strong utility that lifts the whole team, not just personal damage
  • rewards mastery with consistent, sustained output
  • flexible team fit — the its element pairs naturally with several meta teams

Weaknesses & Considerations

Who Should Build Lacrimosa

Lacrimosa is best suited for players who need a dependable damage dealer and are willing to learn its rotation. If you are filling a dps slot for endgame content, Lacrimosa is a dependable choice — especially for accounts without a limited-banner alternative for this role.

Progression Priority

Prioritize its team synergy first, then upgrade the recommended verdant summit and echoes. In combat, Save pulls if you need a self-sustaining DPS. Her lifesteal mechanic may reduce the need for a dedicated healer slot in endgame content.
