Baicang — A Main DPS

AMain DPSStandard banner

Baicang is a solid A-Class DPS with straightforward gameplay. As a greatsword user, he delivers heavy hits that are easy to learn, making him a good pick for new players mastering combat mechanics. While he falls off in late-game compared to S-Class options, he remains a dependable early investment.

Yes. Baicang is a solid A-Class DPS with straightforward gameplay, good for learning combat mechanics. He falls off in late-game compared to S-Class options.
