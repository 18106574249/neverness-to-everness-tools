    • About NTE Tools

    Welcome to NTE Tools — a fan-made database and tools site for Neverness to Everness. We build practical guides, calculators and reference pages so that players can find accurate information quickly, plan their pulls, and enjoy the game without digging through scattered forum threads.

    What we offer

    • Character & weapon guides — builds, recommended gear, teams, and materials for every featured unit.
    • Interactive tools — pull calculators, material trackers, echo scoring and daily checklists.
    • Curated codes & events — up-to-date redemption codes and banner schedules.
    • Beginner guides — step-by-step onboarding for new players.

    Content policy

    All guides are written by the NTE Tools team based on publicly available information, in-game screenshots and community testing. We aim to keep every page accurate and up to date with each game update. Where exact values are unconfirmed, we say so rather than guessing.

    Update schedule

    We refresh guides after every major patch, add new characters and weapons on release, and review the tier list periodically. You can always check the footer or the sitemap for the latest pages.
